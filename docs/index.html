RISM: naming the anonymous

RISM catalogues about 1.6 million music manuscripts and prints; about 297,000 of them are listed under “Anonymus”. Most of those carry an incipit, the opening bars of each movement in Plaine & Easie code. This project searches every anonymous incipit against the attributed ones, keeps matches that no other composer shares, and checks each by hand, movement by movement. So far: 58 confirmed, 1 with conflicting attributions, 7 probable, 4 unresolved. Each row links to the movements side by side. A match means RISM holds an attributed copy of the same music; it does not settle a disputed authorship, and the composers’ printed thematic catalogues may already list some of these copies. Research by Claude (an LLM), with every accepted match compared by eye. Method in METHOD.md.
