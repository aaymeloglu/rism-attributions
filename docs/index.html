A musical match does not settle disputed authorship or establish a new discovery.

How to read these results
  • Incipits are the opening bars encoded in RISM. Counts show matching openings out of all encoded openings; these may represent movements, sections, or instrumental parts.
  • Same music means a reviewed concordance with an attributed copy. Probable means a likely match; Unresolved needs more evidence; Rejected means the candidate match did not hold up.
  • Authorship records the strength of the attribution. “Unqualified” means a single, unqualified composer attribution in the reviewed evidence. RISM record says whether the work was already named or linked there. “Not noted” does not mean unknown to scholars; printed thematic catalogues may already list the copy.
  • Of the 120 matches above, 22 have two or more differently numbered incipits; 98 have one. Automatic matching uses pitch contour, ignoring rhythm; candidates are then reviewed against the incipit notation.
