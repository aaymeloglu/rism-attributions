PL-ŁA RM 212

Catalogued in RISM as “La Cecchina–C major; Manuscript copy; PL-ŁA RM 212” by Anonymus. RISM 1001318287

Piccinni, Niccolò (1728-1800)

Same music

La Cecchina is the other title of Piccinni's La buona figliuola. Its arias match that opera's arias and cavatinas with the same words in several copies, a tone apart: 'Che piacer che bel diletto' (1.4.1, 1.4.2), 'Quel che d'amore si chiama' (1.5.1, as 1.1.2 of RISM 106468), the Aria di Mengotto (1.5.2, as the Cavatina 1.1.1 of RISM 1001266334), the Aria di Sandrina (1.6.1, 1.6.2, as the Scena IV Cavatina 1.13.1, 1.13.2 of RISM 1001281572), the Aria del Marchese (1.7.1, 1.7.2, as 1.16.2 of RISM 1001280681), an Andantino (1.9.1, 1.9.2, as the Aria 1.1.1 of RISM 1001263265), and the Finale I (1.22.1).
