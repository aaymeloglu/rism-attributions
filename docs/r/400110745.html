Favart, Charles Simon (1710-1792)

UnresolvedComposer disputed

Several airs share their tunes, a fourth or fifth apart, with vaudevilles in Favart's opéras-comiques; timbres were reused from work to work, so this shows shared tunes rather than a composer. The anonymous record notes the piece was wrongly attributed to Gluck; the comparators cross-reference Francœur and De Rochet.
