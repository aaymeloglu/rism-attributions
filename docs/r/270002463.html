Catalogued in RISM as “Overtures–D major; Manuscript copy; D-Dl Mus.3556-F-516” by Anonymus. RISM 270002463

Cimarosa, Domenico (1749-1801)

Same music

All three encoded incipits (Allegro assai, Andantino, Allegro con spirito) match the Sinfonia of Cimarosa's L'amor costante in the Dresden score of the same library (Mus.3556-F-512, given as Giulietta e Armidoro in 1790); copies of Il ritorno di Don Calandrino and L'Armida immaginaria open with the same Allegro. The anonymous record names Cimarosa only as the composer of L'infedeltà fedele, into whose overture this sinfonia is bound as a Dresden alternative.
