## Seyffarth: SA 3037 and SA 2957

The anonymous material has seven parts; the attributed comparator, [RISM 469295700](https://rism.online/sources/469295700), has nine. Both first concertante violin parts are available in the Staatsbibliothek's digitized collections:

- [SA 3037, anonymous, permanent viewer](https://resolver.staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/SBB0002C41D00000000), digital identifier PPN1698702582.
- [SA 2957, attributed, permanent viewer](https://resolver.staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/SBB00039B00000000), digital identifier PPN1940612942.
- [SA 2957 title wrapper, scan 1](https://content.staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/dc/1940612942-0001/full/full/0/default.jpg) visibly names Seyffarth. The full personal identity comes from the library's attribution; the wrapper gives the surname.

The copies have differences in layout and expressive markings. “Same music” does not mean a diplomatic note-for-note identity of every part. The evidence now supports retaining the attribution with a stronger basis than the two automatically counted incipits. The displayed count remains two: it measures encoded incipits passing the algorithm, not manuscript passages inspected by a reader.

The library's own catalogue was queried by shelfmark, using its public SRU service (`pica.xsgb="SA 2957"`), alongside the anonymous copy's digital-catalogue metadata. No consulted description establishes that the relationship between these two copies was already published. That is a limited finding, not proof of scholarly novelty.

## Benda: SA 1908 and Mus.2981-N-2

The attributed [Dresden manuscript](https://digital.slub-dresden.de/id307024806), [RISM 212002745](https://rism.online/sources/212002745), is catalogued by SLUB as Franz Benda, LeeB 1.4. Its [IIIF manifest](https://iiif.slub-dresden.de/iiif/2/307024806/manifest.json) supplies the page mapping and catalogue metadata.

The wrapper at scan 1 names Benda. The first-violin Allegro assai starts at scan 5; Larghetto at scan 7; Presto at scan 8. Their rhythms, keys, and opening melodic figures agree with the three RISM encodings used for the anonymous SA 1908 identification. This verifies that the named witness's encoded evidence corresponds to its manuscript. It does not extend the anonymous side beyond its encoded openings.

The Staatsbibliothek catalogue supplies a new digital record for SA 1908 (PPN1974176908), with [permanent link SBB0004800B00000000](https://resolver.staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/SBB0004800B00000000). On the check date that link redirected to a `NOTFOUND` viewer and the IIIF manifest returned 404. The anonymous manuscript therefore could not be inspected.

Douglas A. Lee, *Franz Benda (1709-1786): A Thematic Catalogue of His Works* (Pendragon, 1984), is [catalogued online](https://books.google.com/books?id=0SgKAQAAMAAJ) and [digitized at Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/franzbenda1709170000leed), but its complete text is restricted. The source list for I.4 was not read. Retain the concordance; leave the wider novelty question open.

## Rosetti: Brno A56738 and C62

Günther Grünsteudel's March 2025 [catalogue of editions for the International Rosetti Society](https://rosetti.de/noten/Notenausgaben-2025-3.pdf), printed p. 5, lists C62 as an E-flat clarinet concerto and points to Kunzelmann GM 68 / Octavo 10045. It separately lists a B-flat oboe arrangement of the second movement. This confirms the work-number/key combination independently of RISM, but is an edition list rather than a census of manuscript witnesses.

The anonymous source's three encoded clarinet openings stand a whole tone above the matched comparator encodings. That is compatible with B-flat-clarinet written pitch versus concert pitch, but the actual Brno part was not available to verify its instrument designation or the encoding convention. Do not treat this check as fresh manuscript confirmation of that explanation.

Sterling E. Murray, *The Music of Antonio Rosetti (Anton Rösler), ca. 1750-1792: A Thematic Catalog* (Harmonie Park Press, 1996), is [digitized at Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/musicofantonioro0000murr), with restricted full text. The C62 manuscript source list was not accessible. Whether A56738 occurs there under its current or an earlier shelfmark remains unchecked. No verdict change is justified by this access limitation.

## What follows from the check

Seyffarth is the strongest of these three for a concise catalogue-correction packet, with direct links to matching passages and the attribution on the title wrapper. Benda needs the anonymous manuscript image; Rosetti needs that image and Murray's source list. None of these checks establishes a previously unknown composition, and nothing has been sent to a library or to RISM.
